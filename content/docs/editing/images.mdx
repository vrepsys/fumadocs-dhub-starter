Type `/image` to insert a full-width image block, then upload a file from your computer. Uploaded images land in your repo's `public/img` folder, so they're versioned together with your content.

Plain images are stored as standard Markdown, and Fumadocs optimizes them automatically:

![The Dhub editor](/img/dhub.png)

Click any image on this site to zoom in. The starter enables the Fumadocs `ImageZoom` component for all Markdown images.

## Sizing and captions

## Inline images

For small images that sit inside a line of text, like icons or badges, use `/inline image` instead. Inline images also work inside table cells.
