---
title: Callouts
description: Fumadocs callouts in Dhub, with types and optional custom titles.
---

Callouts highlight information that shouldn't blend into the surrounding text. Type `/callout` to insert one, then use the dropdown on the block to switch its type.

In your repo, callouts are stored as the standard Fumadocs `<Callout>` component, so they render the same everywhere.

## The five types

<Callout type="info">
  Neutral supporting information, like context that's good to know but safe to skip.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea">
  A shortcut or best practice. Use it to point out the easy way to do something.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  Something that can go wrong. Use warnings before steps people commonly get wrong.
</Callout>

<Callout type="error">
  Destructive or irreversible actions. Reserve this one for when it really matters.
</Callout>

<Callout type="success">
  Confirmation that things worked, like a completed setup or a passing check.
</Callout>

## Custom titles

A callout can have its own title instead of the default label:

<Callout type="idea" title="You can rename me">
  Click the title of this callout in Dhub and type your own.
</Callout>

  Insert a callout below with `/callout`, then switch its type using the dropdown on the block.
</Callout>
