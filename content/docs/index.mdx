## How it works

Dhub imports this repository from GitHub, gives you a visual editor with live collaboration, and pushes your edits back, either straight to a branch or as a pull request.

<Callout type="info" title="Nothing proprietary">
  Your content stays plain MDX and your navigation stays in one JSON file. Remove Dhub at any time and the site keeps working.
</Callout>
