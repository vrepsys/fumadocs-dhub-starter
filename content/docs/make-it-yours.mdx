Ready to replace the tour with your own docs? Work through this checklist.

## Checklist

- Rename the site: change `nav.title` in `lib/layout.shared.tsx`

- Replace the pages in `content/docs` with your own MDX files

- Update `navigation.json` to match, or rearrange it visually in Dhub

- Swap `public/img/dhub.png` for your own images

- Pick a theme: change the CSS import in `app/global.css` (Fumadocs ships several presets)

- Deploy, then connect the repository to Dhub and invite your team
