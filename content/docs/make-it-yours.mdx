Ready to replace the tour with your own docs? Work through these steps in order.

## Step by step

<Steps>
<Step>

### Rename the site

Change `nav.title` in `lib/layout.shared.tsx`.

</Step>

<Step>

### Replace the pages

Swap the pages in `content/docs` for your own MDX files.

</Step>

<Step>

### Update the navigation

Edit `navigation.json` to match, or rearrange it visually in Dhub.

</Step>

<Step>

### Swap the images

Replace `public/img/dhub.png` with your own images.

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick a theme

Change the CSS import in `app/global.css`. Fumadocs ships several presets.

</Step>

<Step>

### Deploy and invite your team

Deploy the site, then connect the repository to Dhub and invite your team.

</Step>
</Steps>
